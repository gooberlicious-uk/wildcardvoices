Wildcard Workshops

Singing and performance workshops that get young people belting it out with confidence, led by a professional singing teacher who already works in schools and music schools. Based in the Midlands, we travel to schools, drama schools and youth groups across the region.

  • Perform Like a Pro: one-off singing and stagecraft sessions
  • Half-term singing days
  • Christmas assembly workshops
  • Sessions for drama schools and youth groups

Our workshops are about confidence, creativity and having a go without fear of getting it wrong, so every young person feels able to shine.
