Wildcard Choir

Worcester’s friendliest community choir. A weekly choir for anyone who loves a big song. No auditions, no sheet music and no shame.

Come as you are

Wildcard Voices is a community choir for anyone who loves a big song and a good laugh. Every term is a brand new mixtape of bangers through the eras: the sound of the 60s, 70s glam and Bowie, 80s bops, 90s Ibiza anthems and plenty more.

We teach everything by ear, so you don’t need to read music or have sung before. Loud, shy, off-key or a shower superstar, you belong here. It’s two hours a week that are completely yours, led by a professional singing teacher.

This is about more than singing. It’s a place to build your confidence, meet like-minded people and let go, so you can really come into your own without any judgement.

Together we’ll work on our mixtape and build towards a concert at the end of each term, with the chance to sing with a live band. It’s a real gig, with a real crowd, and you’re the stars of it.

Your questions

Do I need to be able to sing?

No. There are no auditions. If you can hum along to the radio, you’ll fit right in.

Do I need to read music?

Not at all. We teach everything by ear and send practice tracks afterwards.

Do I have to perform in the concert?

No. It’s completely optional, and there’s never any pressure. Come to every rehearsal and sing for the joy of it, or join the concert too. Either way, you’re one of us. Everyone is supported, and nobody is judged.

Can I come on my own?

Yes, plenty of people do, and we’ll make sure you feel welcome from the first minute.

How much does the choir cost?

Your first session is free. After that it’s £45 for a 4-week term (early bird £40), £35 for concessions or £15 to drop in.

Is it right for me?

If you love singing and fancy a laugh, yes. Every age, gender, orientation, ability and background is welcome.
