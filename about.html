About Us

Wildcard Voices is a Worcester music and performance community for people who love to sing, play and perform. We started with a choir, and we’re growing into a home for choirs, singing lessons and workshops for schools and kids.

We believe everyone deserves a space to be themselves and sing out loud. Everything we do is about building confidence, meeting like-minded people and letting go, without judgement. It’s friendly, a bit bonkers and open to everyone, whatever your age, background or experience.

Behind Wildcard

Cerys

Founder, choir leader and singing teacher

Hi, I’m Cerys! I’m a professional actor, singer and singing teacher, and I started Wildcard Voices so everyone can have some properly bonkers fun in their week.

  • Trained in Musical Theatre at the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland
  • Professional actor and singer, with roles in A Christmas Carol and Alice in Wonderland
  • Appeared in the Schwarzkopf Live 2026 campaign
  • Over 10 years teaching across the Midlands, currently at Elgar School of Music and The Elms School
  • DBS checked and an Equity member
